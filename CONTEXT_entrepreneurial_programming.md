## The Philosophy

Three things unlock entrepreneurship: **funding, training, and a marketplace.** All three are rare in poverty. Every program Jerry builds addresses at least one of them.

The target community is **ALICE households** — Asset Limited, Income Constrained, Employed. People who work full time and still can't make ends meet. This is not a charity model. It is a wealth-building model.

**Four Bottom Lines** govern every decision:
1. **People** — Living wages, safe workplaces, dignity in every interaction
2. **Planet** — Know your supply chain, minimize harm
3. **Profit** — Financial sustainability and member wealth-building
4. **Ownership** — Democratic governance, one worker one vote

**The Ethical Floor (non-negotiable):**
- Living wages for all worker-owners
- No exploitation
- Democratic governance
- Transparency
- Community benefit
- Co-ops buy from co-ops

## The Fledge — Programs

### The Entrepreneurial Journey (flagship)
A free, open community pathway for anyone building something. No application. No pitch competition. No gatekeeping. Michigan Small Business Support Hub partner (LEAP + MEDC).

Four stages:
1. **Idea Exploration** — problem identification, community listening
2. **Validation** — customer discovery, market testing
3. **Business Formation** — legal structure, financial literacy, incorporation
4. **Growth** — scaling, hiring, market expansion

Inclusive to: high-tech founders, artists, social entrepreneurs, worker co-ops, anyone with an idea they can't let go of.

Weekly meetups, coaching, workshops on branding, legal structures, financial literacy, and connections to Michigan accelerators.

### 99 Problems but a Pitch Ain't One
Community pitch event, runs every two weeks. 99-second pitch, $99 prize, crowd votes (no judges). Open to anyone anywhere. Rolling submissions.

Why it exists: Standard pitch competitions eliminate people who aren't "polished." This one eliminates that barrier.

### Free Stand
No-questions-asked food access at 1300 Eureka St, Lansing MI 48912. Show up, take what you need. Funded by donations, run by volunteers. Philosophy: basic needs are basic rights.

### FLDG Token
Community currency on the Polygon blockchain. Address: `0x5118aec3afcca3f1e21733ee9c88bb800afe6f7b`. Exchange rate: 25 FLDG = 1 hour of work. Used at Urbandale Farm (25 FLDG/hr earned) and accepted at the Music Studio.

### Urbandale Farm
Community garden where members work and earn FLDG. Produce goes to the Free Stand and the community.

### Music Studio
Professional recording/production open to community members. Accepts FLDG. Philosophy: creative expression is community infrastructure.

### Fledge Base + Coding Meet-up
Device access, digital literacy training, tech support. Coding meet-up for all skill levels. Addresses digital exclusion as a barrier to employment, healthcare, and education.

### The Entrepreneurial Journey (8 stages, digital version)
Starts at idea, ends at a worker-owned cooperative. At **Stage 4** there is a fork: Traditional Business path OR Worker Co-op path.

Tools unlock as you progress:
- Problem statement builder
- Customer persona tool
- Contact manager
- Deal pipeline
- Financial ledger
- Co-op governance templates

Peer support and Fledge coaching available at every stage.

### The Co-op Handbook (13 Parts)
The backbone of the Worker Co-op Builder. A founding group works through all 13 sections to produce a complete co-op business plan.

1. **Front Matter** — Name, tagline, service area, industry, core values, ethical commitments, incorporation timeline
2. **Founding Story** — Who we are, what problem we saw, who we serve, why cooperative, 10-year vision, 90-second story
3. **Ownership Community** — Founding group, member buy-in, payment terms, probationary period, education program, year 3 targets
4. **Who We Serve** — Worker-owner commitments, 3 customer profiles, value proposition, market size, community impact
5. **Channels** — How customers find you, monthly costs, reaching future members, co-op ecosystem connections
6. **Relationships** — How worker-owners treat each other, customer trust standards, retention targets, community accountability
7. **Branding** — Three defining words, brand story, visual identity, content plan, marketing budget
8. **Four Bottom Lines** — People/Planet/Profit/Ownership specifics: living wage target, max wage ratio, environmental commitments, surplus allocation, governance model
9. **Resources** — Equipment, space, inventory, legal/incorporation costs, tech stack, insurance, startup capital total, funding sources
10. **Partners** — Supplier partners, collaborators, community anchors, other co-ops, local sourcing targets
11. **Revenue, Pricing, Key Activities** — Products/services, pricing, cost per unit, monthly projections, Year 1-3 revenue targets, break-even month, financial risks
12. **Legal & Governance** — State of incorporation, legal entity, ICA Seven Principles in practice, board composition, decision-making, dispute resolution, patronage distribution
13. **Financial Plan** — Month 1 P&L, surplus distribution, Year 1 totals, risks and mitigation

The handbook produces **10 output documents**: full co-op business plan, financial projections, governance bylaws draft, member agreement, brand guide, and others.

### ICA Seven Cooperative Principles (referenced throughout)
1. Voluntary and open membership
2. Democratic member control
3. Member economic participation
4. Autonomy and independence
5. Education, training, and information
6. Cooperation among cooperatives
7. Concern for community

## GAIA Solutions — Worker-Owned Software Cooperative

GAIA Solutions builds the technology that powers the ecosystem. It is itself a worker-owned cooperative — structure matches mission.

### Four Capability Areas

**1. Civic & Investigative Tech**
OSINT tooling, investigative data platforms, public records pipelines, surveillance mapping, narrative visualization. For journalists, advocates, and communities that need rigorous tools.

**2. Management Systems & Auditing**
ISO 9001, ISO 22000, FSSC 22000, automotive/aerospace quality systems. Internal audit programs, gap analysis, corrective action, compliance software. "Auditor's rigor applied to full stack."

**3. Data Platforms & Public Diagnostics**
Community-scale data infrastructure. Civic prediction platforms, public health dashboards, economic diagnostics, open data pipelines. Built to be maintained, not just launched.

**4. Cooperative & Community Infrastructure**
Co-op formation tools, federated network infrastructure, mutual aid platforms, community-governed digital spaces. "We build with, not for."

### Six Operating Principles
1. **Binding over voluntary** — structural accountability, not good intentions
2. **Auditor's rigor** — evidence-based, traceable, documented
3. **Open source default** — build in the open
4. **No extractive pricing** — sliding scale for co-ops and mutual aid orgs
5. **Build with, not for** — internal capacity-building, not dependency creation
6. **First among equals** — equal stakes, equal voice, equal accountability

## How the Ecosystem Connects

```
Person shows up at The Fledge
    → enters the Entrepreneurial Journey (in-person or via resilience.foundation)
    → gets coaching, peer support, tools that unlock per stage
    → at Stage 4: chooses Traditional path or Worker Co-op path
    → if Co-op path: builds using the 13-part handbook on resilience.foundation
    → resulting co-op may become a GAIA Solutions client or partner
    → co-ops buy from co-ops → ecosystem grows
    → surplus stays in the community instead of leaving to investors
```

The model is explicitly anti-extractive. No equity taken. No investor returns. No gatekeeping pitch panels. Wealth builds in the hands of the people doing the work.
